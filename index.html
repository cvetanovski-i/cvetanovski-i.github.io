Clutch Cuts

Clutch Cuts posts short gameplay highlights: the best moments of a level or mission, cut into vertical clips that end on the win.

Clutch Cuts Uploader

Clutch Cuts Uploader is a private tool run by the channel owner on their own computer. It is not offered to the public and has no other users. It:

  • uploads the channel's own gameplay clips to the Clutch Cuts accounts on YouTube (and, when connected, TikTok, Facebook and Instagram) on a posting schedule;
  • reads view and watch-time statistics for those videos, so the owner can see which clips perform best.

It only ever accesses the Clutch Cuts accounts that the owner signed in with.
