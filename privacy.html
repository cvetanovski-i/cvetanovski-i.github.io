Privacy Policy

Clutch Cuts Uploader · Last updated 16 November 2026

Clutch Cuts Uploader ("the app") is a personal tool used only by the owner of the Clutch Cuts channel to publish their own videos and read their own statistics. This policy explains what data it accesses and how it is handled.

Data the app accesses

  • Sign-in tokens issued by YouTube / Google (and, if connected, TikTok and Meta) after the owner approves access.
  • Channel and video information for the owner's own account: video IDs, titles, upload status.
  • Aggregate statistics for the owner's own videos: views, watch time, likes, shares, subscribers gained.

The app does not access any other person's account or data, and collects no data from viewers.

How the data is used

Only to upload the owner's videos and to show the owner how those videos perform, so future clips can be improved. Data is not used for advertising and is not sold.

Storage and sharing

All data is stored locally on the owner's computer. The app has no servers. Data is not shared with or transferred to any third party, except the platforms themselves (YouTube, TikTok, Meta) when the app calls their official APIs.

Google API Services

Clutch Cuts Uploader uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; see also the Google Privacy Policy. The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Tokens and statistics are kept until the owner deletes them. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google), in TikTok under Settings → Security → Apps, or in Facebook under Settings → Apps and websites. Revoking access stops all further data access; locally stored data is then deleted by the owner.

Contact

Questions about this policy: the contact email on the Clutch Cuts YouTube channel's About page.
